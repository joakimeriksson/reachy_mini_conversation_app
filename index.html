Local Assistant

Talk, see, and move together — on your own hardware.

A friendly, camera-aware companion for Reachy Mini that runs entirely on your own machines. Speech, reasoning and vision go through Ollama; the voice through a self-hosted server. No cloud, no API keys, nothing leaves your network.

What’s inside

All-in-one conversational layer for your robot

The app blends realtime speech, vision, and motion so Reachy Mini feels present..

🎤

Natural voice chat

Talk freely and get fast replies from a local Gemma model that hears your voice directly — and talk over Reachy to interrupt it.

🎥

Vision-aware replies

Ask the camera tool to see what’s in front, track a face, or keep attention on whoever is speaking.

💃

Expressive motion

Queue dances, play recorded emotions while Reachy listens and talks.

🧠

Personalities on demand

Switch conversation styles through profiles and decide which tools (dance, camera, tracking) each persona can use.

🔒

Private by construction

Works with wired or wireless Reachy Mini. Speech, vision and voice all run on hardware you control — put the models on a beefy machine and keep the robot thin.

How it feels

From hello to helpful in seconds

  • 👋 Say “Hey Reachy” and start chatting—no extra setup in the moment.
  • 👀 Ask what it sees; it can peek through the camera or keep focus on your face.
  • 🎭 Trigger emotions or dance breaks to keep the conversation lively.
  • 📝 Follow along with the live transcript on the app's settings page.

Where it shines

Great for demos, teaching, and playful exploration

Show off how Reachy Mini listens, responds, and moves in sync. Whether you’re guiding a class, hosting a booth, or experimenting at home, the app keeps the robot expressive without juggling scripts or joystick controls.
